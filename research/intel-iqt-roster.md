# In-Q-Tel public roster

Compiled from Wikipedia's Investments section, the Intercept 2016 table, Dealroom holdings, TipRanks, and Gaebler. Regenerated by `scripts/build_iqt_roster.py`.

Washington Post (2016): IQT listed 325 investments and **more than 100 were secret**. This file is the public remainder. A roster edge is a cheque (or a database listing a cheque), not operational control of the company.
