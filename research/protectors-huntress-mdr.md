# Huntress: SMB/MSP managed detection, 24/7 SOC, owned EDR stack

Huntress is a **managed** security vendor, not an enterprise EDR console in the CrowdStrike sense. The product is the SOC: owned Managed EDR, Managed ITDR on Microsoft 365 identities, Managed SIEM, and awareness training, staffed 24/7.

**Why it is on this map.** Most of the protector layer so far is either (a) the EDR *class* (CrowdStrike, Defender) or (b) public-sector sharing (CISA). Huntress is the missing **SMB/MSP** node: the same EDR/identity controls, delivered as a service to shops that will never staff a SOC. That is a different concentration risk (one MSP, many tenants) and the same class failure (BYOVD still kills the agent).

**Graded.** Vendor existence, product list, SOC, MSP channel, and CNA status are public-record. Endpoint/identity headcounts are vendor claims. No Huntress-specific BYOVD or helpdesk-bypass case is asserted here; those edges inherit the *class* (EDR_XDR, Helpdesk_Identity).
