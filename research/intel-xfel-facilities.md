# XFELs and Xarion

Three different things:

**Xarion Laser Acoustics** (Vienna, IQT roster) is an *optical microphone* / laser-ultrasound sensor. Not an accelerator. Not lithography.

**SACLA** (SPring-8 Angstrom Compact FEL) is RIKEN's hard XFEL at Harima, Japan. Ultra-short, intense X-ray pulses for atomic structure, ultrafast chemistry, materials. Same campus as SPring-8.

**European XFEL** ([xfel.eu](https://www.xfel.eu/)) is the 3.4 km superconducting linac from DESY Hamburg to Schenefeld. Highest repetition-rate hard XFEL. Science users, 12 member states.

**LCLS** (SLAC), **SwissFEL**, **PAL-XFEL** sit on the same physics family so the globe is not Japan-and-Germany-only.

**xLight** is an *industrial* FEL light source aimed at EUV scanners. Same electron-to-light trick; different customer (fabs, not beamline users). **Substrate** claims X-ray lithography in a foundry, not a user hall.
