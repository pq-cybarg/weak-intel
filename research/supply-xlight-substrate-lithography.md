# xLight and Substrate: US particle-accelerator lithography bets against the ASML/TSMC stack

Two different companies. Same missing layer on this map: **who might break the EUV/foundry duopoly**, and with whose money.

**xLight** is a *light source*. Free-electron laser, utility-scale, meant to feed ASML scanners rather than replace them. CHIPS/Commerce equity LOI (up to $150M, Dec 2025). Gelsinger as chair. 2028 Albany prototype. ASML has said LPP remains preferred.

**Substrate** is a *foundry plan* that happens to invent a lithography tool (X-ray via compact accelerators). Backed by Founders Fund, General Catalyst, and **In-Q-Tel**. Claims 2nm-class XRL and much cheaper wafers. No high-volume fab. 2028 is a target, not a run rate.

Neither is proven in high-volume manufacturing. They belong next to ASML, TSMC, GlobalFoundries, and the CHIPS/intel-capital stack, not as a third monopoly.
