# Snyk: developer-first SCA/SAST/container/IaC on the software supply chain

Snyk is **AppSec in the developer workflow**, not a SOC and not an EDR agent. SCA (Open Source), SAST (Code), Container, and IaC scan what gets *built*; Huntress watches what *runs* on SMB endpoints. Both are protectors. They are not substitutes.

**Why it is on this map.** Recurring targets here include software-update trust and supply-chain implants (SUNBURST, npm worms). Snyk is the commercial control that sees the dependency graph. It works on *known* bad versions and licenses. It does not stop a trusted maintainer shipping a first malicious build, and it does not see a trojanized *signed vendor update* (SolarWinds).

**Aug 2026.** Snyk's Trust Center documents Snyk identifying an active npm supply-chain campaign (Keyv/Cacheable, "ChainDrop"). That is Snyk as an ecosystem observer, not a claim that Snyk originated or was the first victim of that campaign.
