# Executive summary

Weak.Intel is a bubble-map of the cyber/operator landscape.

**Thesis:** one dual-use stack, not two armies. Recurring targets are concentration
points. A CRQC reprices harvested public-key ciphertext (HNDL) and classical
signatures (TNFL). Blue-team methods that still work do so by leaving that
substrate or shrinking blast radius.

**Run:** `bash run_all.sh` then open `docs/index.html`.

**Do not read as:** an accusation, a classified leak, or a complete catalog.
It is a study object — macro and micro stories are meant to emerge from the mesh.
